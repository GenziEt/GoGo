# GENZI Monetization — v0.2.7

## Purpose
GENZI now has a zero-cost, monetization-ready creator economy layer. It uses internal **GENZI Coins** so the product can be tested before connecting a real payment provider.

## Features
- Creator tips
- Creator subscriptions
- Paid posts/content purchases
- Wallet ledger and transaction history
- Creator earnings metrics
- Owner-only promotional coin grants
- XP rewards for monetization participation

## Revenue safety
GENZI Coins are not cash and are not presented as withdrawable money. Promotional grants are owner-controlled. No paid API or payment service is required for this release.

## Future payment adapter
A future payment adapter can credit verified purchases after a provider confirms payment. Keep payment-provider credentials server-side, use idempotency keys, and never trust client-supplied payment success.
